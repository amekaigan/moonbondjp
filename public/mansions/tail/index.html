Questions about the Tail Mansion

What is the Tail Mansion in Shukuyō?

The Tail Mansion (尾宿, Bi-shuku) is one of the 27 lunar mansions of Shukuyō, Japan's moon astrology. Moonbond calls it The Finisher: Once you start, you do not stop.

What is the Tail Mansion like in love?

Serious and single-minded, you pursue one person wholeheartedly.

Which mansions are the best match for Tail?

In Shukuyō the luckiest bond is Prosperity & Kinship. For Tail, that means Willow, Drawn Bow, Heart, Winnowing Basket, Stride and Granary.

What jobs suit the Tail Mansion?

Tail is often drawn to Skilled trades, Sport and Research. You save steadily and invest for the long term. Patience is your edge with money; the slow, boring plan works beautifully for you.

How do I know if I am a Tail Mansion?

Your mansion comes from where the Moon stood on your birthday in the old Japanese lunar calendar. Enter your birthday in Moonbond's free reading to see yours in seconds.

How do I find my mansion?

Your mansion comes from where the moon was on the day you were born, counted on the old Japanese lunar calendar. You only need your birthday. No birth time or birthplace is needed. The free test on Moonbond works it out for you and for anyone you are curious about.
