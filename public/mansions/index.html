Tradition and our own touches

From the Shukuyō tradition

The 27 mansions, how your birthday maps to one, the six bonds and their near, middle and far distances. Most English names, such as Rooftop, Willow and Dipper, follow the usual English names of these lunar mansions in East Asian astronomy. A few are adapted to read more naturally.

Moonbond's own touches

The scores, the kimono and obi colors, the job and friendship notes, and the wording of every reading. They are written to make the tradition easy to enjoy, not as part of it.
