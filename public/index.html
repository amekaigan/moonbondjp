            What is Shukuyō astrology?

            Shukuyō (宿曜), also written Sukuyō, is a Japanese form of astrology built on 27 lunar mansions. Its source text reached Japan from Tang China in the 9th century.

            How is it different from a Western moon sign?

            A Western moon sign places the Moon in one of 12 zodiac signs at your exact birth time. Shukuyō divides the Moon's path into 27 mansions and reads yours from your birthday on the old Japanese lunisolar calendar, so no birth time is needed.

            What is it best at?

            Relationships. The system sorts any two mansions into six kinds of bond, which is why it is widely used in Japan for love, friendship and work matches.

            Is this a prediction?

            No. It does not tell the future. Enjoy it as a living Japanese tradition, and as a way to look again at yourself and the people you love.
