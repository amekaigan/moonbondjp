Two sides of one bond

In Shukuyō, a pair is read from both directions. One of you sees the other as Risk, and the other sees you as Achievement.

危

Ki · Risk

The Wild Card

Different worlds and different values. Exciting and unpredictable, better for adventures than routines.

成

Sei · Achievement

The Sharpener

They sharpen your thinking and push you to finish what you start. A strong match for work and study.

What tends to happen between you

  • You are often amazed at how differently they see things.
  • Trips and special days go great. Ordinary Tuesdays are harder.
  • The same argument about money, time or plans keeps coming back.
  • You get more done when they are around.
  • You wanted comfort. You got advice.
  • You do not want to look lazy in front of them.
One thing to tryWhen you disagree, ask "why does that matter to you?" before you argue the point.

Near, middle or far

The same bond feels different depending on how far apart the two mansions sit on the wheel.

Near4 steps apart. The pull is felt fast and strong.
Middle5 steps apart. A steady pull with room to breathe.
Far13 steps apart. A gentle pull that works slowly, over years.
